## 1. Data controller

The controller of personal data of Service users is Superwizor sp. z o.o. (the **Controller**), registered in Poland.

> **Document status.** Draft — will be reviewed by the DPO before commercial launch.

## 2. Categories of data processed

The Controller processes the following data categories:

- **Account data** — email address, first and last name, therapeutic modality.
- **Organisation data** — legal name, address, administrator details.
- **Session data** — therapy-session audio recordings, transcripts, reports.
- **Technical data** — IP address, device identifier, login timestamp.

## 3. Legal basis and purposes

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Service delivery | Art. 6(1)(b) — contract performance |
| Invoicing | Art. 6(1)(c) — legal obligation |
| Marketing of own services | Art. 6(1)(a) — consent |
| Security and abuse prevention | Art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest |

## 4. Data location

All data is stored in Google Cloud Platform infrastructure in the `europe-central2` region (Warsaw). Data is not transferred outside the European Economic Area.

## 5. Retention

- Account data — for the duration of the contract plus 6 years after termination (tax obligations).
- Audio recordings — by default 30 days from session end, unless the User selects a longer retention period in settings.
- Transcripts and reports — for the duration of the contract, until the User manually deletes them.

## 6. User rights

Users have the following rights, which can be exercised in the order listed:

1. access to data (Art. 15 GDPR),
2. rectification (Art. 16),
3. erasure ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17),
4. restriction of processing (Art. 18),
5. data portability (Art. 20),
6. objection to processing (Art. 21),
7. complaint to the Polish DPA (uodo.gov.pl).

These rights can be exercised by emailing `dpo@superwizor.ai`.

## 7. Security

Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Access to clinical data is restricted to authorised therapists. AI models **are not trained on User data** — details in the [DPA](/en/legal/dpa).
