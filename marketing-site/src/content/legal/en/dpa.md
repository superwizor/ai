## 1. Parties

This Data Processing Agreement (the **DPA**) is concluded between:

- the **Controller** — the User or Organisation using the Service,
- the **Processor** — Superwizor sp. z o.o.

> **Document status.** Draft — will be reviewed by legal counsel before signing with B2B customers.

## 2. Subject of processing

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Nature of processing | Transcription of recordings, report generation, hosting |
| Purpose | Delivery of Superwizor AI to the Controller |
| Categories of subjects | Patients, therapists, supervisors |
| Categories of data | Audio recordings, transcripts, session identifiers |
| Duration | Term of the master agreement |

## 3. Processor obligations

The Processor:

1. Processes data only on documented instructions from the Controller.
2. Ensures persons authorised to process data are committed to confidentiality.
3. Implements technical and organisational measures consistent with Art. 32 GDPR.
4. Assists the Controller in fulfilling data-subject rights.
5. Returns or deletes data at the end of services (Controller's choice).
6. Makes available the information needed to demonstrate Art. 28 GDPR compliance.

## 4. Sub-processors

Current sub-processor list:

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Google Cloud Platform (GCP) | Hosting, storage, KMS | europe-central2 (Warsaw) |
| Firebase Authentication | Authentication | europe-west |

Changes to the sub-processor list require 30 days' prior notice to the Controller.

## 5. No model training

The Processor confirms that Controller data **is not used to train AI models**. Agreements with model providers (Anthropic, Google) include explicit training opt-out.

## 6. Transfers to third countries

All data is stored in the EU. Should a transfer outside the EEA become necessary in future, the Controller will be notified in advance and the transfer will only proceed under the EU Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC).

## 7. Data breach

The Processor notifies the Controller of a personal-data breach within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, following the procedure in Art. 33 GDPR.

## 8. Audit

The Controller has the right to one audit per year, with 14 days' prior notice, during working hours and without disrupting the Processor's operations.
