# Superwizor AI App Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-06-01**

## Introduction

### Purpose and Scope of the Policy
This Privacy Policy ("Policy") defines the rules for processing the personal data of Professional Users and Patients in connection with the use of the Superwizor AI mobile and/or web application ("App"), provided by Euphire sp. z o.o. ("Service Provider", "We").

The purpose of the Policy is to ensure transparency and fulfill the information obligations arising from Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 ("GDPR"). The Service Provider is committed to protecting the privacy of all individuals whose data it processes and ensuring compliance with GDPR regulations.

### Definitions of Key Terms
For the purposes of this Policy, the following terms have the following meanings:
* **App:** The Superwizor AI software in mobile (iOS, Android) and/or web versions, serving as a tool to support Professional Users in analyzing therapy and coaching sessions.
* **Service Provider:** Euphire sp. z o.o., with its registered office at ul. Odrzańska 10a/48, Kraków, Poland, registered under KRS number 0000907254, NIP 6793219020.
* **Professional User (Professional):** A psychoanalyst, psychologist, psychiatrist, therapist, coach, group practice, or other organization providing mental health or personal development services.
* **Patient:** A natural person whose personal data is processed by the Professional User in connection with the services they provide, using the App.
* **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
* **Health Data:** Personal data relating to the physical or mental health of a natural person revealing information about their health status (Art. 4(15) GDPR).
* **Transcription:** The automatic text record of an audio recording, generated by speech recognition technology.
* **Clinical Report:** A structured document automatically generated by artificial intelligence based on the session transcription.
* **HiTOP:** The Hierarchical Taxonomy of Psychopathology — a dimensional symptom assessment system whose measurements the App generates based on transcriptions.
* **Processing:** Any operation or set of operations performed on personal data.
* **Data Controller:** An entity that, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data.
* **Data Processor:** An entity that processes personal data on behalf of the controller.
* **Envelope Encryption:** An encryption technique where data is encrypted with a unique data encryption key (DEK), and the DEK itself is encrypted with a master key (KEK) managed by Cloud KMS. This provides a double layer of protection.

### 5. Technical and Organizational Measures

The Service Provider implements advanced security measures appropriate to the high risk associated with processing special category data (health data):

**Data residency and region control:**
* The Service Provider's entire infrastructure is located in the **europe-central2 (Warsaw, Poland)** region of Google Cloud Platform.
* Region restrictions are enforced at the GCP organizational policy level (constraint `gcp.resourceLocations`), preventing the deployment of resources outside the permitted region.
* The only exception is the Vertex AI service (used for report generation and embeddings), located in the **europe-west4 (Netherlands)** region — still within the European Economic Area (EEA). The Speech-to-Text service uses a dedicated European endpoint (`eu-speech.googleapis.com`).

**Encryption at rest:**
* **CMEK (Customer-Managed Encryption Keys):** All infrastructure services (Cloud Storage, Cloud SQL, Secret Manager) use encryption keys managed by the Service Provider in Cloud KMS (keyring `superwizor-keyring`), with automatic rotation every 90 days.
* **Envelope Encryption:** All special category data (transcriptions, clinical reports, HiTOP measurements, RAG clinical memory) is encrypted at the application level using the AEAD algorithm. Each record has a unique data encryption key (DEK), which is encrypted with a master key (KEK) managed in Cloud KMS. This means that even if access to the database is obtained, the data remains unreadable without access to Cloud KMS.

**Encryption in transit:**
* All connections use TLS/SSL protocol.
* Inter-service communication uses gRPC (HTTP/2) with encryption.

**Network isolation:**
* The database (Cloud SQL PostgreSQL) has no public IP address — this is enforced by GCP organizational policy (`sql.restrictPublicIp`).
* Database access is exclusively via VPC Connector on a private network.

**Access control:**
* Each microservice has a dedicated Service Account with minimum permissions (principle of least privilege, Zero Trust).
* Creation of service account keys is blocked by organizational policy (`iam.disableServiceAccountKeyCreation`) — CI/CD access is exclusively via Workload Identity Federation.
* User authentication is via Firebase Authentication (supported methods: e-mail/password, Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In).

**Audio recording lifecycle:**
* Audio recordings are automatically and permanently deleted from Cloud Storage within **48 hours** of upload, regardless of the processing outcome. This is implemented via the Object Lifecycle Management (OLM) mechanism at the GCS bucket level.
* Deletion of audio after successful transcription occurs immediately; the 48-hour OLM serves as a fail-safe backup for failed attempts.

**Data deletion and audit:**
* User and Patient data is deleted via a soft-delete mechanism (marking with `deleted_at`), ensuring the audit trail required by the GDPR.
* Permanent deletion occurs 30 days after marking.
* Every significant data operation is recorded in an audit events table.

**Monitoring and testing:**
* Continuous monitoring of logs and metrics via Google Cloud Logging and Cloud Monitoring.
* Regular security reviews of infrastructure and code.

## Part II: Information for Patients

### 1. Who is Responsible for Your Data?
The Data Controller of your data within your therapy or coaching sessions is your therapist/coach/doctor. They decide what data is collected, for what purpose they use the Superwizor AI App, and are responsible for informing you about the recording of the session.

### 2. Who Processes Your Data?
Euphire sp. z o.o. (App Provider) processes your data solely as a Data Processor — on behalf of and upon the instruction of your therapist/coach/doctor. We do not use your data for any of our own purposes.

### 3. What Data is Processed and How?
As part of your therapist's use of the App, the following data is processed:
1. **Audio recording** of your session — it is uploaded to encrypted servers in the European Union (Warsaw) and is **automatically and permanently deleted within 48 hours**. After this period, recovering the recording is not possible.
2. **Transcription** (text record of the conversation) — it is generated automatically by speech recognition technology. Speakers in the transcription are labeled with neutral labels (e.g., "Person 1", "Person 2") — without indicating roles. The transcription is encrypted and stored in encrypted form.
3. **Clinical report** — it is generated automatically by artificial intelligence based on the transcription. It contains session analysis and dimensional measurements. It is available exclusively to your therapist in read-only mode (they cannot edit it in the App). The report is encrypted.
4. **Anonymized session summary** — a short, de-identified summary (without names, surnames, or place names) may be retained in encrypted form as "clinical memory" to help your therapist maintain continuity of care between sessions.

Your data is accessible exclusively to your therapist — no other User of the App has access to it.

### 4. Security of Your Data
Your data is protected with advanced encryption technologies:
* Recordings, transcriptions, and reports are encrypted both during transmission (TLS/SSL) and storage (envelope encryption with keys managed in Google Cloud KMS).
* Audio recordings are automatically deleted within 48 hours.
* All data is stored exclusively on servers located in the European Union (Warsaw, Poland).
* Deletion of your therapist's account results in cascading deletion of all associated Patient data.

### 5. Your Rights
You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and port your data. To exercise your rights, contact your therapist/coach/doctor, who is the Controller of your data and is directly responsible for fulfilling your rights.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — the President of the Office for Personal Data Protection (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland).
