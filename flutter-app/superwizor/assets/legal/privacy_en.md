# Privacy Policy for the "Labirynt Premium" App

## Introduction (TL;DR)

Your data is processed by Euphire Sp. z o.o.

We collect a minimum of technical data necessary for the App to function. We also collect data that you voluntarily provide – profile pictures, anniversary dates, souvenir trip photos, and messages from the internal chat.

We never sell personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

You have the full right to access your data (we will export it from the server for you upon request). You also always have the option to permanently delete your Account on your own.

## § 2. Purposes and Legal Bases for Data Processing

We process your data on the basis of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Electronic Communications Law (PKE) for the following purposes:

**Account Management and User-Generated Content (UGC):** We process your identifier, email address, encrypted password, progress in the App, as well as any content you voluntarily upload, including: profile pictures, souvenir trip photos within the calendar, anniversary date entries, and message history on the internal chat. This is necessary to deliver the full functionality of the App and fulfill the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

**Duo Mode Support:** To synchronize the game, calendar, and chat with your partner, family member, or friend, we process identifiers linking your Account to the assigned person's Account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

**Premium Payment Verification:** Using the authorized RevenueCat system, we analyze anonymized transaction tokens from Apple/Google stores to grant you access to paid features (Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

**Technical Analytics and Security:** We diagnose the App's stability through anonymous crash reports (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics) based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log recording on the device occurs after system-level consent is granted.

**Push/Email Communication (Marketing):** In-app notifications (including the "Question of the Day" module) are sent exclusively based on your voluntary consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which can be withdrawn at any time in your smartphone settings.

## § 3. Privacy Protection and Special Category Data in Duo Mode

Due to the nature of the App (conversations and building relationships), the answers, trip photos, or chat messages you add may in some situations indirectly indicate information legally treated as special category data (e.g., relating to intimate relationships, health, or worldview).

By entering a partner's code and authorizing the connection in Duo Mode, the User gives explicit and voluntary consent to the processing of such data and its display on the paired partner's/friend's device (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).

This data is shared exclusively with the chosen partner. The User may disconnect at any time in the App's settings, which immediately stops the mutual sharing of information to the partner's device.

## § 4. Data Recipients and Transfer Outside the EEA

The Controller does not sell personal data. We entrust their processing exclusively to trusted cloud technology providers:

a) Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC – Firebase infrastructure (database management, authentication, and secure storage of multimedia files);

b) RevenueCat Inc. – validation of digital subscriptions.

If, for technological reasons, data is transferred to servers outside the European Economic Area (e.g., to the USA on Google servers), this is done based on legal mechanisms approved by the European Commission, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC).

## § 5. Data Retention Period

Profile data, saved photos, calendar history, chat messages, and activity are processed until the User requests the deletion of their Account.

Upon initiating the Account deletion function, the files and data associated with it are permanently erased from production databases without undue delay.

Billing information (subscription transaction history in the form of anonymized logs) must be archived by the Controller for tax purposes for a period of 5 years, counting from the end of the calendar year in which the tax payment deadline expired.

## § 6. User Rights

Under the GDPR, Users are entitled to the following rights:

The right to request access to their data and to obtain a free copy (upon the User's request, the Controller will export a JSON/CSV system file from the Firebase platform and securely send it to the User's email address within 30 days);

The right to rectification (correction) of inaccurate data;

The right to erasure of data ("right to be forgotten");

The right to restriction of processing;

The right to object to processing based on legitimate interest;

The right to easily withdraw given consents (e.g., for "Question of the Day" notifications);

The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (the President of the Personal Data Protection Office in Warsaw).

## § 7. IT Security Measures

The Controller applies rigorous organizational and technical safeguards. Communication with the cloud is secured by cryptographic protocols (TLS/SSL), which protect the transmission of private messages and photos, and User passwords are subject to one-way hashing, making it impossible to read them in plain text.
